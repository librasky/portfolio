Research

Exploring spatial interfaces, AI systems, and human-centered XR experiences.

My role as a XR designer and educator is really exciting. Being able to experience cutting-edge technology is cool. I realize that I am studying and keep updating to the state of the art in the latest technology evolution. My journey as a creative academic, working with my students enables me to dream more about the future. I have sensed a lot of possibilities in adding value to the VR experience. Watching and working together with young students makes me surprised. A lot of cool new ideas come from them.
